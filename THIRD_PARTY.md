## English

AstroWeb application code is MIT-licensed, Copyright 2026 Ron Hoebe. This does not relicense user photographs, FITS files, external captions or catalog data. Media and private observation data are excluded from Git.

The star catalog reader is adapted from **RAHoebe/RaspAstroDisplay**, MIT, Copyright 2026 RAHoebe; its complete notice is retained in `astroweb/catalog/RaspAstroDisplay-LICENSE.txt`. The Hipparcos TSV, Stellarium western JSON and source description are unchanged copies from that repository. Hipparcos is ESA/CDS catalog data, not MIT application code. Stellarium text/data is CC BY-SA as specified in the retained description; no constellation illustrations are included. See the source URLs above and `astroweb/catalog/README.md` for provenance and data terms.

Astropy/ERFA performs FITS and sky calculations using bundled IERS data with automatic downloads disabled. Python dependencies and the separately installed FFmpeg/libx264 encoder retain their own licenses. NASA captions preserve attribution and provenance, and do not imply permission to reuse third-party images. The gallery uses the user's own photographs.

No renderer source was copied from DeepParallax, Zenith, DepthFlow, Seiza, Depthy or parallax-designer. They are research references only. AstroWizard is neither bundled nor modified; AstroWeb reads the user's exported files.

## Hemelreis / Sky journey

**ESO/S. Brunier** — [The Milky Way panorama, eso0932a](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [ESO usage terms](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/). De gepubliceerde 6000 × 3000 JPEG is lokaal opgenomen, licht opgehelderd en voor de film herprojecteerd naar een ovale hemelkaart. The distributed panorama is a tone-adjusted derivative. AstroWeb reprojects it to a Hammer sky map and animates the camera, capture frame and transition to the original photograph.

**ESA/Gaia/DPAC** — [Gaia DR3](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3), [AIP Gaia Archive](https://gaia.aip.de/). De vier ICRS-hoeken zijn gemeten op de sterrenexport die bij de oorspronkelijke final hoort. Measurements use a local triangle match followed by a TAN fit to Gaia DR3 stars brighter than G=14. Coordinates are stored in display order (top left, top right, bottom right, bottom left) and tied to the SHA-256 of the unchanged final export. NGC_7000 matched 414 stars, median residual 1.13 arcsec. HIP_106890 matched 442 stars, median residual 0.99 arcsec. These values describe the fit at matched stars.

**Digitized Sky Survey / STScI / NASA · CDS · IRSA** — [DSS2 colour HiPS and full survey credits](https://alasky.cds.unistra.fr/MocServer/query?ID=CDS%2FP%2FDSS2%2Fcolor&get=record&fmt=html), [IRSA mirror](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/hips/CDS/DSS2/color/properties), [MAST data use](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use). De Hemelreis gebruikt lokaal gecachte uitsneden van de blauwe en rode fotografische hemelplaten. The Digitized Sky Surveys were produced at STScI under US Government grant NAG W-2166 from photographic data obtained using the Oschin Schmidt Telescope on Palomar Mountain and the UK Schmidt Telescope. Colour composition and HEALPix tiling by CDS, HiPS copyright CNRS/Unistra, database licence ODbL-1.0. Local mosaics are reprojected and blended with the wide-field panorama. [Gaia DR3 at AIP](https://gaia.aip.de/metadata/gaiadr3/gaia_source/) supplies catalogue coordinates and G magnitudes for automatic upload alignment.

**Pan-STARRS1 / CDS** — [DR1 colour HiPS (i, r, g), credits and data provenance](https://alasky.cds.unistra.fr/MocServer/query?ID=CDS%2FP%2FPanSTARRS%2FDR1%2Fcolor-i-r-g&get=record&fmt=html), [MAST mirror](https://stpubdata.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/mast/skybackgrounds/PanSTARRS/properties). AstroWeb gebruikt deze onafhankelijke optische waarnemingen voor het IC 1396-veld, waar de DSS2-achtergrond evenwijdige strepen bevat. Observation credit: PS1 Science Consortium. HiPS by CDS, CNRS/Université de Strasbourg, ODbL-1.0. Local mosaics are reprojected for the film.

**NGC 6992** — [NASA Science / APOD](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2009-december-1-ngc-6992-filaments-of-the-veil-nebula/), [NASA Caldwell 33](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-caldwell-catalog/caldwell-33/), [ESA/Hubble](https://esahubble.org/news/heic0006/). Tweetalige eigen samenvatting / original bilingual summary. Additional catalogue identifications use CDS Sesame/SIMBAD.

**Bovenaanzicht / Face-on map** — [Our Milky Way Gets a Makeover, PIA10748](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA10748), NASA/JPL-Caltech/R. Hurt (SSC/Caltech). The locally bundled WebP is a resized version of the unlabelled artist’s reconstruction. The Sun is placed at (0.50, 0.69) in normalized image coordinates using the [annotated map](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso1339e/) as a reference. The map adopts approximately 26,700 light-years from Sun to Galactic centre. Sky directions come from CDS Sesame/SIMBAD. The initial landmarks use NASA distance estimates. The expanded atlas uses the sources below. These projected positions and the illustrated spiral arms have different uncertainties. De objectnamen worden bij het afspelen getekend en zijn afzonderlijk uitschakelbaar.

## Aanvullende kaartlabels / Additional atlas labels

The local atlas-landmarks.json file contains separate attributed catalogue subsets. Coordinates are in decimal degrees, and available heliocentric distances are converted from parsecs or kiloparsecs to approximate light-years. Missing or placeholder distances are excluded from the face-on map. These data retain their source terms separately from the application code.

- **HYG 4.1** (source S), David Nash / Astronexus. Named objects, coordinates and valid distances from [HYG Database](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database/tree/main/hyg/CURRENT). Extracted, rounded and reformatted under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **OpenNGC** (source O and field_objects), Mattia Verga and contributors. Coordinates, designations and common names extracted from the NGC and addendum tables. Filtered, reformatted and partly translated under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [Original dataset and notices](https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC).
- **Milky Way globular clusters** (source H), William E. Harris (1996, AJ, 112, 1487; December 2010 edition). Positions and heliocentric distances in Part I of the [original catalogue](https://physics.mcmaster.ca/~harris/mwgc.dat). The catalogue is supplied free of charge; redistribution must refer to that original source and must be free of charge. [HEASARC documentation](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/W3Browse/all/globclust.html).
- **CDS Sesame/SIMBAD** (source C). Additional local nebula, cluster and stellar positions resolved by their catalogue identifiers using [CDS Sesame](https://cds.unistra.fr/cgi-bin/Sesame). Where a NASA distance is supplied separately, its URL is recorded in the object entry.

**M31 / NGC 224** — [NASA · Messier 31](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-31/) en [ESA · M31 the Andromeda Galaxy](https://www.esa.int/media/m31-the-andromeda-galaxy). Eigen tweetalige samenvatting / original bilingual summary. The discovery overlay also uses the OpenNGC object classifications under the same catalogue licence cited above.

**AllWISE** — [IRSA AllWISE colour HiPS, survey properties and full acknowledgements](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/hips/CDS/AllWISE/RGB-W4-W2-W1/properties). Red = W4 (22 μm), green = W2 (4.6 μm), blue = W1 (3.4 μm). These data products are from WISE (UCLA and JPL/Caltech) and NEOWISE (JPL/Caltech), funded by NASA. HiPS colour tiles from CDS via IRSA, reprojected to the capture for display. Reported arcseconds per pixel refer to the display mosaic sampling at the top edge; survey angular resolution depends on band.
**Galaxy environment** — OpenNGC positions and object classes as credited above. Confirmed associations and selected distances from the NASA Hubble Messier catalogue: [M32](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-32/), [M110](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-110/) and [M51](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-51/). Further object research via NASA/IPAC NED. Catalogue positions alone do not establish physical membership.
