Image and film credits

The gallery combines original astrophotography, rendered AstroWeb films, published mission images and recordings of the English web and TV interfaces. Processing includes resizing, cropping, camera animation and overlays.

Original captures and processing

Ron Hoebe: IC 1396, NGC 7000, NGC 6992, M31 and the visible-light Sun. The Andromeda layer comparison uses aligned exports from AstroWizard. The AstroWeb software is MIT-licensed. The software licence applies to the code.

Survey and Hubble imagery

  • Milky Way panorama: ESO/S. Brunier, eso0932a, CC BY 4.0. Tone-adjusted, reprojected and animated.
  • Optical sky journey: Digitized Sky Survey, STScI, based on photographic data from the Oschin Schmidt and UK Schmidt telescopes; HiPS by CDS. See the DSS2 colour record.
  • Elephant’s Trunk flight: original IC 1396 capture by Ron Hoebe. Registered optical detail: Pan-STARRS1 Surveys (PS1) / STScI / CDS.
  • Andromeda mosaic: NASA, ESA, J. Dalcanton, B. F. Williams, L. C. Johnson, the PHAT team and R. Gendler. NASA Messier 31 · ESA/Hubble reference image.
  • Infrared comparison: AllWISE, NASA/JPL-Caltech, IRSA and CDS.

Solar System and nightscapes

  • Solar detail clip: solar prominences during totality in Dallas, 8 April 2024, NASA / Keegan Barber. Opening capture: Ron Hoebe.
  • Moon and Apollo 11: NASA/JPL/USGS; NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
  • Star trails over La Silla, G. Lambert/ESO. A milky arc over Paranal, ESO/G. Brammer. Both under CC BY 4.0; used as single-image pan/zoom examples.
